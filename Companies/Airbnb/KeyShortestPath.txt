Problem :

You are given an m×n grid containing walls (#), open cells (.), a single start cell (@), up to six 
keys labeled 'a'–'f', and matching doors 'A'–'F' that can be passed only after obtaining their 
corresponding key. From any cell, you may move one step up, down, left, or right to an in-bounds 
non-wall cell. Return the minimum number of steps required to collect all keys, or -1 if it is 
impossible. Handle m, n up to 30. Describe your state representation, search strategy, pruning, and 
how you detect revisits efficiently. Provide time and space complexity and key test cases.
