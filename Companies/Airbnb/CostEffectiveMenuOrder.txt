Problem : Most Cost Effective Menu Order

You are building an app that helps users determine the most cost-effective order they can place at a
restaurant for the food items they want.

You are given:

A menu containing individual items and combo deals (bundles of items at a discounted price)
A list of items the user wants to eat
Your task is to find the minimum cost to obtain all wanted items and return which menu items to order.

Rules:

1. Each wanted item must be obtained exactly once (no duplicates needed)
2. You can order any combination of menu items (individual or combos)
3. Combo items provide multiple items at a single price

Input:
menu = [
  [5.00, ["pizza"]],
  [8.00, ["sandwich", "coke"]],
  [4.00, ["pasta"]],
  [2.00, ["coke"]],
  [6.00, ["pasta", "coke", "pizza"]],
  [8.00, ["burger", "coke", "pizza"]],
  [5.00, ["sandwich"]]
],
wanted = ["sandwich", "pasta", "coke"]

Output:
min_cost = 11.00
orders = [["sandwich", "coke"], ["pasta"]]
// or: [[8.00, ["sandwich", "coke"]], [4.00, ["pasta"]]]

Explanation:

To get sandwich, pasta, and coke:
Option 1: Buy sandwich+coke combo (8.00) + pasta (4.00) = 12.00
Option 2: Buy sandwich (5.00) + pasta (4.00) + coke (2.00) = 11.00 ← Optimal
The minimum cost is 11.00.
