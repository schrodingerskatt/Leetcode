PROBLEM : Maximize reward by scheduling jobs

Given n jobs, each with a start time, end time, and reward, choose a subset of non-overlapping 
jobs to maximize total reward. Return the maximum reward and one optimal set of job indices.

Constraints: n up to 100,000; times within 32-bit or 64-bit integers; rewards non-negative. Aim for 
O(n log n) time and near O(n) space. Explain your algorithm (e.g., sorting, binary search, and DP), 
prove correctness, analyze complexity, and discuss edge cases such as identical start/end times and 
zero-length jobs.
